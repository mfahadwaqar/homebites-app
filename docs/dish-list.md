# HomeBites dish list for photo sourcing

Save each photo as the filename shown (JPG, about 1000 px wide) in `static/catalog/food/real/`, overwriting the current one. Then update that dish's entry in `catalog/data/photo_sources.json` (author, source_url, license, license_url) and its row in `docs/food-photo-credits.md`. Reload the server after changing the JSON.

Photos in the app should be free to reuse with credit (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain) or your own.
